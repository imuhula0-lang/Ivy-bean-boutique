Brewing Equipment

Everything you need for a good old home brew, from french presses to espresso machines. Each product contains simple tips to help you get started.

French Press

A classic glass brewer. Your coffee steeps in hot water, giving you a rich, full-bodied cup.

Best for:Medium to coarse grind

How to Use

  • Use 1 part coffee to 15 parts of water.
  • Steep your coffee for about 4 minutes, then press slowly.
  • Pour your coffee out immediately so it doesn't get bitter.

Ksh 1000

Pour-over Dripper

A simple ceramic cone, giving you full control of your brewing. It produces a clean and bright cup, showing of light roasts.

Includes:Dripper + Filters

How to Use

  • First rinse the filter using hot water.
  • Pour a small amount of your coffee and let it "bloom" for about 30 seconds.
  • Pour water over it in small circles until you reach your desired amount.

Ksh 700

Espresso Machine

Make espresso, lattes and capuccinos in your kitchen with this compact home espresso machine.

Features:Steam wand, pressure controls

How to Use

  • Warm up the machine for about 15 minutes before brewing.
  • Aim for a shot which takes about 25 to 30 seconds.
  • Make sure to wipe the steam wand after every use.

Ksh 15,000

Burr Grinder

Grinds coffee beans for a tasteful cup, from fine espressos to a coarse French press.

Type:Adjustable burr grinder

How to Use

  • Grind coffee beans just before you brew.
  • Grind finely if the coffee tates sour and coarsely if it tastes bitter.
  • Remove old grounds atleast once a week.

Ksh 4,500

AeroPress

It makes a tasteful cup in 2 minutes. Great for camping and road trips.

Best for:Travel and single cups

How to Use

  • Use it upside-down so nothing drips early.
  • Stir for about 10 seconds, then press gently for about 30 seconds.
  • You can add hot water for an Americano-style.

Ksh 2200

Moka Pot

This moka pot makes strong, espresso-style coffee without the need for an expensive machine.

Best for:Medium-fine ground and dark roast

How to Use

  • Fill the bottom part with hot water, just below the valve.
  • Level the coffee gently. Do not tamp it.
  • Once it boils, take it off the heat.
